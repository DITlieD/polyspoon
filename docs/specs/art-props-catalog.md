# Chest and crate construction candidate

The chest keeps its existing body width/depth/height, arched lid-rise profile, body course count, band inset, latch toggle, three material roles and rear-X hinge range of 0–110 degrees. The hollow body stays fixed. The first candidate gives its wood edges a restrained profile, constructs the curved cover from supported shell boards and end cheeks, and gives straps, hinge journals and the focal hasp distinct functional thicknesses.

Every added cover, cheek, rim or hinge collar attached to the lid joins `lid-pivot`. Static mounting plates, journals and the latch receiver remain on the body. Bands derive their underside from the actual lid profile. The hasp attaches to a lid front rail, and the keyhole plate seats on the hasp. Surface-ray tests check these relationships through closed, open and boundary poses. Bounds alone cannot establish support. Runtime posing and saved `lidAngle` retain their existing distinction.

The crate keeps its silhouette, open/closed construction, boards, braces and wood/timber material hierarchy. The original inspection pegs can float ahead of their supporting front rims. The bounded repair seats those pegs against the existing rim surface while retaining their readable heads. No other crate decoration is proposed.

Affected paths are the contributed chest builder and legacy crate builder. Registry, canonical semantics, shared geometry/material helpers, controls, configuration, migrations, palettes and published files remain unchanged. Both feed the existing catalog/browser/CLI/MCP/module/export paths. New geometry stays unpublished until later version work.

Tests cover actual strap-to-cover and hasp/hinge support, moving membership, hollow-body access, existing parameter failures, extrema and crate peg seating on both faces and both frame weights. Existing color inputs and defaults stay authoritative. Invalid keys, out-of-range dimensions and unsupported opening values still reject. No animation, physics, randomized wear or aesthetic acceptance is implied.

## Chest target metadata correction

Dimension controls declare key-specific affected part prefixes. Width includes base, feet, body/end boards, rim, cover and band/hinge assemblies. Depth also includes the front latch/keyhole assembly. Body height excludes base/feet. Lid rise covers the curved lid, cheeks and lid straps. Band inset covers straps and hinge/collar placement. The latch toggle covers the hasp, receiver and keyhole. Existing lid-angle targets remain unchanged and cover the moving lid collars. Prefixes may include unchanged members of a named assembly.

Production geometry, transforms, materials, parsing, non-target control fields and versions must remain identical before and after this correction, including boundary inputs. Changed-part tests compare actual world-space mesh payloads and require every changed or removed part to match a declared prefix. The same targets must survive the existing GLB export/import path. Missing keyhole and collar targets must fail the check.
