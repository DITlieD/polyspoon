## Research decisions

Keep the existing TypeScript/Three.js recipe, catalog and verification core. It already has lofts, chamfered profiles, beams, roof construction, stone shapes, seeded variation and typed controls. Extend those paths where original asset designs demonstrate a gap. Do not replace them with another generic geometry framework.

The recommended architecture separates reusable construction helpers, family recipes, portable delivery and platform services. A family recipe owns its silhouette and construction logic. Shared helpers supply consistent profiles, connections, material roles and constraints. The same validated design drives the browser, CLI, local MCP, procedural module, GLB and engine adapters. The marketplace owns identity, immutable versions, review and access, rather than geometry rules.

This preserves synchronous browser editing and existing saved designs. It requires explicit recipe authoring for new object families; no finite helper library guarantees every requested object looks good. A richer Blender/ProcFunc/CAD path may later produce baked assets, but does not automatically compile to a live JavaScript generator. Keep baked/static submissions distinguishable from parametric recipes.

Use ordinary exportable materials as the baseline. Add original optional detail tiers only when they preserve silhouette and improve a named asset at its intended view distance. TSL is an optional renderer capability, not a mandatory dependency or a promise of GLB shader portability. Baking may help a demonstrated surface-detail need; neural texture systems, training, inverse procedural models and a second runtime are deferred experiments, not launch dependencies.

Geometry checks, visual judgment and engine import checks remain distinct. The cited VLM paper reports judge agreement on a bounded dataset, not universal correctness. Use consistent views and structured findings. Add independent judging or additional views for uncertainty; do not require 24 views and two paid model calls for every small change. Do not enforce watertightness on intentional openings or foliage, auto-fill holes, or treat material names as proof of imported appearance.
